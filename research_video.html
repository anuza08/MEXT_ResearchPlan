1
Building the training dataset — three sources
Every 10 days, the paddy contributes to the dataset from above and below:
🚁
Drone Camera
How it's collected: a multispectral camera mounted on a drone flies a fixed grid over the paddy — capturing canopy images we label for training.
🛰️
Satellite Imagery
How it's collected: downloaded free from JAXA G-Portal (GCOM-C, ALOS-2), ESA Sentinel-2, and NASA Landsat — geo-clipped to the same plot.
📡
IoT Sensors
How it's collected: tiny field-deployed sensors log soil moisture, leaf wetness, temperature & humidity every 10 minutes — streamed via LTE-M.
